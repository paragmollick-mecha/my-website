AeroMark ME 366 Project

AeroMark is an FPV drone-based wildlife management system designed to remotely tranquilize and safely immobilize wild animals for research and conservation, rescue, and veterinary support. The drone carries a lightweight electromagnetic coil gun and a stabilized camera for accurate targeting. The coil gun works by boosting a 12V supply to high voltage, charging capacitors, and discharging them through a solenoid to generate a magnetic field that launches a metal dart. The system reduces direct human-animal interaction, improves researcher safety, and enables efficient field operations such as animal tagging, monitoring, and health assessment. The drone has a flight time of approximately 10 minutes, a range of 1 km, and manual control with an FPV camera system. Future developments include AI-assisted targeting, sensor upgrades, automatic dosing, swarm operations, and advanced data logging. Current limitations include limited range and accuracy, dependence on weather conditions, the need for skilled operators, and potential GPS signal issues in dense environments.
